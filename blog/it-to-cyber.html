From IT operations to cybersecurity

DHANESH RAAJ THANAMURTHI · FIELD NOTES

My experience in IT operations has shaped the way I think about cybersecurity. Before looking at a system as a security problem, it helps to understand how the system is used, maintained and supported.

Learning through operations

At Shydas Cafe, my responsibilities included day-to-day IT support, network services, POS systems, equipment maintenance, troubleshooting, inventory planning and staff support. This work developed practical habits: isolate an issue, understand the environment, test changes carefully and communicate the solution clearly.

Connecting infrastructure with security

Security depends on the reliability and visibility of underlying systems. Familiarity with networks, operating systems, services and user workflows provides useful context when approaching security challenges.

My areas of interest

I am particularly interested in security monitoring, log analysis, threat investigation, Linux systems and network security.
