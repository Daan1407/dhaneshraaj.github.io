CASE STUDY / FINAL YEAR PROJECT

How I approached ILATCS

DHANESH RAAJ THANAMURTHI · FIELD NOTES

Intelligent Log Analysis and Threat Correlation System (ILATCS) was my final-year project at Asia Pacific University. It reflects my interest in cybersecurity operations, security events and the challenge of turning technical information into something useful for investigation.

Project focus

The project focused on log analysis and threat correlation. The aim was to support a more structured way to examine security-related events and identify relationships that may be relevant during an investigation.

Investigation workflow

SECURITY EVENTS → LOG ANALYSIS → EVENT CORRELATION → INVESTIGATION

What I took from the project

Working on ILATCS helped me connect cybersecurity concepts with system-level information. It strengthened my interest in security monitoring, event analysis and the practical reasoning involved in investigating suspicious activity.
