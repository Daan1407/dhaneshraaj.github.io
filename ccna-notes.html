NETWORKING / LEARNING

Lessons from CCNA fundamentals

DHANESH RAAJ THANAMURTHI

Networking knowledge has repeatedly helped me understand problems beyond the network itself. Learning the fundamentals gave me a better mental model for how systems communicate.

What stayed with me

  • Understanding addressing and routing before troubleshooting higher-level services.
  • Separating connectivity problems from application problems.
  • Thinking about traffic flow rather than treating a network as a black box.
  • Using structured troubleshooting instead of changing multiple variables at once.

Security connection

Network visibility is also security visibility. Knowing what normal communication should look like makes unusual traffic easier to reason about.
