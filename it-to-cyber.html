From IT support to cybersecurity

DHANESH RAAJ THANAMURTHI

My path into cybersecurity has not been limited to security tools. Hands-on IT support taught me how systems actually behave when users depend on them every day.

Infrastructure first

At Shydas Cafe, I worked with network services, POS systems, computers, peripherals, troubleshooting and general technology operations. That experience built practical habits: isolate the problem, understand the system, document the fix and consider what could prevent the issue next time.

Why that matters for security

Cybersecurity sits on top of infrastructure. Understanding operating systems, networks, services and user workflows makes security concepts more concrete.

Where I am heading

My current interests are SOC operations, threat detection, Linux security, log analysis and security research. I want to keep combining practical infrastructure knowledge with security thinking.
